# Course Curriculum: Digital Literacy & Data for Life 2026
**Project:** โครงการสร้างการรับรู้ด้านดิจิทัลของประชาชนในระดับจังหวัดและอำเภอ พ.ศ. 2569
**Target:** Leaders, Farmers, and General Citizens

---

## Pillar 1: Digital Literacy (การรู้เท่าทันดิจิทัล)

### Module 1: Digital Citizenship and Generative AI Mastery
*Duration: 90 Minutes*

#### **Lesson 1.1: Digital Intelligence (DQ)**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Digital Identity:** Managing your online presence responsibly.
    - **Digital Emotional Intelligence:** Empathy and etiquette in online communication.
    - **Critical Thinking:** Identifying fake news, deepfakes, and misinformation.
- **Activity:** "Fact or Fake" Challenge – Real-world examples of news for participants to verify.
- **Outcome:** Participants can navigate digital spaces with high awareness and responsibility.

#### **Lesson 1.2: AI Utilization (ปัญญาประดิษฐ์)**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Introduction to GenAI:** What is ChatGPT/Gemini and how do they work?
    - **Daily Productivity:** Using AI to summarize long documents, write emails, or plan farm schedules.
    - **Prompt Engineering:** How to ask AI effectively to get the best results.
- **Activity:** "AI Assistant" Workshop – Asking AI to help solve a specific daily problem (e.g., writing a request to local government).
- **Outcome:** Participants understand AI as a tool for efficiency, not a replacement for human judgment.

---

### Module 2: Digital Security and PDPA 2026
*Duration: 75 Minutes*

#### **Lesson 2.1: Digital Security (ความมั่นคงปลอดภัย)**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Online Threat Scenarios:** Call center scam tactics, phishing links, and malware.
    - **Safe Habits:** Two-factor authentication (2FA), strong passwords, and safe Wi-Fi usage.
    - **Incident Response:** What to do if you've been scammed (hotlines and legal steps).
- **Activity:** "Scam or Safe" – Analyzing SMS and email templates to identify red flags.
- **Outcome:** Participants can identify and avoid common cyber threats.

#### **Lesson 2.2: Personal Data Protection Law (PDPA)**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Citizen Rights:** Right to access, right to be forgotten, and right to object.
    - **Data Privacy in Daily Life:** Why you shouldn't share IDs or bank details publicly.
    - **Consent:** Understanding what you agree to in apps and services.
- **Activity:** Reviewing a simple privacy consent form to identify what data is being collected.
- **Outcome:** Participants understand their legal rights regarding personal data.

---

## Pillar 2: Data Literacy (การใช้ข้อมูล)

### Module 3: Power of Open Data for Local Development
*Duration: 90 Minutes*

#### **Lesson 3.1: Accessing Open Government Data**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Public Portals:** Introduction to data.go.th and NSO's official statistical portals.
    - **Local Statistics:** Finding crop prices, rainfall data, or community demographics.
    - **Reliability Factors:** Checking the update frequency and source of data.
- **Activity:** "Data Treasure Hunt" – Finding one specific statistic relevant to their province or district.
- **Outcome:** Participants can independently locate and verify public government data.

---

### Module 4: Data-Driven Decision Making Workshop
*Duration: 90 Minutes*

#### **Lesson 4.1: Planning and Problem Solving with Data**
- **Sub-topics:**
    - **Data Interpretation:** Turning numbers into stories and insights.
    - **Evidence-based Planning:** Using past trends to predict future needs (e.g., planting cycles).
    - **Visualization Basics:** Understanding simple charts and graphs.
- **Lesson 4.2: Actionable Decisions**
    - **Case Study:** Using market price data to decide when to sell products.
- **Activity:** **The "Decision Lab" Workshop**
    - Participants work in groups to solve a local scenario (e.g., "Improving crop yield" or "Boosting local tourism") using a provided data set and the "Data-to-Action" worksheet.
- **Outcome:** Participants can apply data insights to real-world planning and decision-making.

---

## Assessment Strategy
- **Baseline:** Pre-test (Kahoot) covering all 4 modules.
- **Formative:** Real-time polling (Slido) during lectures.
- **Final:** Post-test (Kahoot) + Group Workshop evaluation (Module 4).
- **Recognition:** Certificate of Digital & Data Proficiency.
