# Curriculum and Content Plan (TOR 3.1.1)
**Project:** Digital Literacy Training Project 2026 (สำนักงานสถิติแห่งชาติ)

## Overview
This curriculum is designed to enhance digital intelligence and data usage skills among Thai citizens, leaders of communities, and farmers.

---

## Pillar 1: Digital Literacy (การรู้เท่าทันดิจิทัล)
### Module 1: Digital Citizenship and Generative AI Mastery
*   **Topic 1: Digital Intelligence (DQ)**
    *   Developing skills for safe and effective digital interaction.
    *   Critical thinking for digital consumption.
*   **Topic 2: AI Utilization (ปัญญาประดิษฐ์)**
    *   Introduction to Generative AI.
    *   Practical tools and prompt engineering for everyday use.
    *   Ethical AI usage.

### Module 2: Digital Security and PDPA 2026
*   **Topic 3: Digital Security (ความมั่นคงปลอดภัย)**
    *   Protecting against online scams and call center threats.
    *   Safe online habits and password management.
*   **Topic 4: Personal Data Protection Law (PDPA)**
    *   Understanding citizen rights under PDPA 2026.
    *   Best practices for data privacy.

---

## Pillar 2: Data Literacy (การใช้ข้อมูล)
### Module 3: Power of Open Data for Local Development
*   **Topic 5: Accessing Open Government Data**
    *   Navigating public data portals.
    *   Identifying reliable sources and statistics.
*   **Topic 6: Reliable Statistics**
    *   How to interpret and verify data sources.

### Module 4: Data-Driven Decision Making Workshop
*   **Topic 7: Planning and Problem Solving with Data**
    *   Using data to identify community problems.
    *   Evidence-based planning for agriculture or local business.
*   **Topic 8: Data-Driven Decision Making**
    *   Translating data results into actionable decisions.
