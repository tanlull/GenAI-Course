# Master Project Specification: Digital Literacy Training 2026
**Version:** 1.1 (Consolidated)
**Client:** สำนักงานสถิติแห่งชาติ (National Statistical Office - NSO)
**Project Goal:** To provide a comprehensive data set for AI agents to generate training content, marketing materials, and logistical plans.

---

## 1. Project Identity & Metadata
- **Project Name (TH):** โครงการสร้างการรับรู้ด้านดิจิทัลของประชาชนในระดับจังหวัดและอำเภอ พ.ศ. 2569
- **Project Name (EN):** Digital Literacy for All & Data for Life (Provincial/District Level 2026)
- **Slogan:** "Data is Opportunity, Digital is the Skill of the Future!"
- **Scale:** 18+ Sessions Nationwide | 100+ Participants per Session | 1,800+ Total Reach.
- **Duration:** 1-Day Onsite Workshop (approx. 6-7 hours).
- **Target Audience:** Community leaders, educational personnel, farmers, and the general public.

---

## 2. Curriculum Architecture (TOR 3.1.1)

### Pillar 1: Digital Literacy (การรู้เท่าทันดิจิทัล)
| Module | Key Topics | Learning Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M1: DQ & GenAI Mastery** | Digital Intelligence (DQ), Generative AI Tools, Prompt Engineering | Participants can use AI for daily tasks and navigate the web with "Digital Intelligence." |
| **M2: PDPA & Cyber Security** | Cyber Scam Protection, Call Center Threats, PDPA 2026 Rights | Participants can protect themselves from fraud and understand their data privacy rights. |

### Pillar 2: Data Literacy (การใช้ข้อมูล)
| Module | Key Topics | Learning Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M3: Open Data Access** | Public Data Portals, Government Statistics, Data Reliability | Participants can find and verify reliable public data for local use. |
| **M4: Data-Driven Decision** | Problem Solving with Data, Evidence-based Planning | Participants can use data to make informed decisions for agriculture or business. |

---

## 3. Training Schedule & Methodology (TOR 3.1.3)
**Primary Method:** Active Learning (Lecture + Workshop Ratio 50:50)
**Interactive Tools:** **Kahoot** (Gamified Quizzes), **Slido** (Real-time Q&A/Polls)

- **08:30 - 09:00:** Registration & Pre-test (**Kahoot**)
- **09:00 - 10:30:** Module 1: DQ & GenAI Mastery (Interactive Lecture)
- **10:45 - 12:00:** Module 2: PDPA & Cyber Security (Case Studies)
- **13:00 - 14:30:** Module 3: Open Data Exploration (Demo/Practical)
- **14:45 - 16:15:** Module 4: Data-Driven Decision Making (**Hands-on Workshop**)
- **16:15 - 16:45:** Summary & Post-test (**Kahoot**)

---

## 4. Trainer & Resource Requirements (TOR 3.1.2 & 3.1.4)

### Trainer Profile
- **Expertise:** Must match modules (DQ, AI, PDPA, Data Science).
- **Qualifications:** Hold certificates (IC3/ICDL/etc.) or have published academic work/portfolios in these fields.
- **Experience:** 3+ years in facilitation; skilled in digital engagement tools (Kahoot/Slido).

### Required Materials
- **Manuals:** 
    1. *EDC Plus Handbook* (Digital Citizenship)
    2. *Go Cyber Handbook* (Cyber Safety for Seniors)
- **Digital Assets:** Presentation slides, pre-configured Kahoot quizzes, Slido sessions.
- **Physical Assets:** Data-Driven Decision Making Worksheets, PDPA Quick Guides.
- **Award:** Digital Certificates for successful completion.
