# Teaching Materials and Manuals (TOR 3.1.4)
**Project:** Digital Literacy Training Project 2026

## List of Materials
The following materials are modern, easy to understand, and designed for broad accessibility.

### 1. Primary Manuals (Digital & Print)
- **EDC Plus Handbook (Digital Citizenship):** A comprehensive guide on navigating the digital world safely. (Reference: `EDC-Plus-Handbook_website_18_oct_S.pdf`)
- **Cyber Safety Handbook (Senior Citizens):** A localized, simplified guide for cyber safety and scam protection. (Reference: `คมอคนไทย-Go-Cyber-ฉบบวยเกา.pdf`)

### 2. Digital Assets
- **Presentation Slides:** Visual-heavy slides for each module (DQ, GenAI, PDPA, Data Literacy).
- **Interactive Quizzes:** Pre-configured Kahoot templates for Pre-test and Post-test.
- **Slido Sessions:** Pre-configured polling rooms for participant interaction.

### 3. Practical Handouts
- **Data-Driven Worksheet:** A physical or digital template for the Module 4 workshop to guide participants through a decision-making exercise.
- **PDPA Quick Guide:** A 1-page summary of rights for participants to keep.

### 4. Certification
- **Digital Certificates:** Provided to all participants who complete both the training and the Post-test.
