# Trainer Requirements (TOR 3.1.2)
**Project:** Digital Literacy Training Project 2026

## Profile: National Expert Trainer (วิทยากรผู้เชี่ยวชาญ)

### Qualifications
1.  **Professional Certification:** Must hold a certificate in Digital Literacy (e.g., IC3, ICDL, or equivalent) or Data Science/Analytics.
2.  **Academic Excellence:** Must have academic work, research, or a portfolio related to:
    *   Digital Intelligence (DQ).
    *   AI and Emerging Technologies.
    *   Cybersecurity or Personal Data Protection (PDPA).
    *   Data Analytics or Open Data utilization.
3.  **Experience:** Minimum 3 years of experience in facilitating workshops for diverse groups (community leaders, elderly, or farmers).
4.  **Skills:** Proficient in using active learning tools like Kahoot and Slido to maintain participant engagement in an onsite setting.

### Documentation Required
- Proof of Certification (matching curriculum topics).
- Academic work or Portfolio (demonstrating expertise in 3.1.1).
